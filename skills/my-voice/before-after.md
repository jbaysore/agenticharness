## Opinion: ranked systems

Task: answer whether ranked systems make competitive games more or less fun.

Actual GPT opening: "Ranked systems in competitive games can make gaming more exciting, but they can also take away some of the enjoyment."

Positive reference: C:\Users\Joshua\Projects\Agentic Harness\skills\my-voice\samples\opinion-ranked-systems.txt

Read the full genuine answer. Josh commits to a position while qualifying its subjectivity. He distinguishes his own motivation from benefits that could matter to other people, and explains the mechanism in linked clauses. The GPT answer introduced stress and a balanced conclusion that Josh did not choose to argue. Preserve the user's actual stance rather than automatically adding that structure.

This example supports neither a ban on concessions nor a requirement to use the same personal/other-people distinction in every piece. Other originals include objections, factual explanation and conclusions. Its brevity also does not override a future user's explicit word range.
